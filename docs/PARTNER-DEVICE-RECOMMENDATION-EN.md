# SnackCheck — Partner device recommendation (EN)

**Stand:** 2026-08-10 · WP-HW2 · “We recommend, you buy”

We do **not** sell, warranty, or RMA kitchen tablets. We recommend a shortlist; the customer purchases from their preferred retailer.

## What to buy

- **10″ Android tablet (Wi‑Fi), Android 10+**, preferably with a wall-mount / VESA kit.
- Street range typically **€120–€250** for consumer units; industrial / PoE kiosk tablets **€300–€700**.
- Refurbished business tablets + VESA are fine for LOI (**€80–€180**) if MDM/lock-task is available.

Named class guidance and AT/CH notes: see `DEVICE-SHORTLIST.md` in this app’s docs.

## Before go-live (customer IT)

1. Mount the tablet at fridge height; keep it on wall power.
2. Enable lock-task / dedicated-device mode so only **SnackCheck Kitchen** runs.
3. Pair once with the `snkterm_…` token from SnackCheck → Settings → License (token shown once).
4. Staff unlock with **PIN** (or QR / badge wedge). No phone app.
